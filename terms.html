What the apps do and do not do

  • Retention Guard moves tickets to the Freshdesk Trash and never deletes permanently. Restoring from the Trash is possible until Freshdesk empties it.
  • Bulk Update Pro changes the ticket fields you specify. Bulk Update Pro for Freshservice changes the ticket fields you specify and posts the private notes you add. In both, undo restores previous values only on fields that still hold the value the job wrote, and does not remove notes.
  • Send Later posts replies and notes at the time you choose and skips them in the cases shown in the app.
  • Recurring Tickets creates tickets from the templates you save, at the scheduled times or when you click Run now. It does not edit or delete existing tickets.
  • Ticket Checklists adds checklists to tickets and, when you turn it on, a private note when a checklist is completed. Blocking Resolve and Close applies only to the ticket page, not to bulk actions, automations or the API.
  • Privacy Requests deletes only the contacts you select or confirm. Delete moves a contact to Deleted contacts, where it can be restored; Erase permanently deletes the contact and all their tickets and cannot be undone. Deciding whether a privacy request is valid, and sending the export to the person, are your responsibility.
  • Ticket Approvals records approval requests and decisions as private notes and, when you choose statuses, changes the ticket status. It does not issue refunds or credits, and it does not close or delete tickets. Deciding what to approve is your responsibility.
  • App Reviews creates tickets from public store reviews and adds private notes. It posts a developer response to Google Play or the App Store only when an agent sends a public reply on a review ticket, and it does not delete tickets or reviews. What you post as a developer response must follow the store's own policies.
  • Business Reviews creates tickets from public Google reviews of the locations you pick and adds private notes. It posts an owner reply on Google only when an agent sends a public reply or posts from the sidebar on a review ticket, and it does not delete tickets, reviews or replies. It needs your own Google Cloud project with Business Profile API access approved by Google. What you post as an owner reply must follow Google's own policies.
  • Ticket Snooze changes the status and tag of the tickets you snooze and, when you turn it on, adds private notes. At the wake time it changes a ticket only if it is still in the snooze status. It does not reply to customers and does not close or delete tickets. Wake times depend on the Freshworks scheduling service and can be a few minutes late.
  • The apps depend on Freshdesk, Freshservice and the Freshworks platform, App Reviews also on Google Play and App Store Connect, and Business Reviews also on Google's Business Profile APIs. If they are unavailable, rate limited or change their API, an app may be delayed or stop working until it is updated.
