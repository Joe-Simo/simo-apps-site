What the apps do and do not do

  • Retention Guard moves tickets to the Freshdesk Trash and never deletes permanently. Restoring from the Trash is possible until Freshdesk empties it.
  • Bulk Update Pro changes the ticket fields you specify. Bulk Update Pro for Freshservice changes the ticket fields you specify and posts the private notes you add. In both, undo restores previous values only on fields that still hold the value the job wrote, and does not remove notes.
  • Send Later posts replies and notes at the time you choose and skips them in the cases shown in the app.
  • Recurring Tickets creates tickets from the templates you save, at the scheduled times or when you click Run now. It does not edit or delete existing tickets.
  • Ticket Checklists adds checklists to tickets and, when you turn it on, a private note when a checklist is completed. Blocking Resolve and Close applies only to the ticket page, not to bulk actions, automations or the API.
  • Privacy Requests deletes only the contacts you select or confirm. Delete moves a contact to Deleted contacts, where it can be restored; Erase permanently deletes the contact and all their tickets and cannot be undone. Deciding whether a privacy request is valid, and sending the export to the person, are your responsibility.
