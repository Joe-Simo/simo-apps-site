SLA Policies Plus support

Contact

Email: joesimo@mail.grokbot.com

Support is by email only. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include your Freshdesk domain, a ticket id, the policy name and a screenshot of what you see. Never send your API key.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app change Freshdesk's own SLA or due dates?

No. The app keeps its own targets and its own clock. Your default Freshdesk SLA keeps running for every ticket, and the app only adds private notes and, if you choose, tags.

How does the app pick a policy for a ticket?

Policies are checked in the order you set, and the first enabled policy that matches wins. A ticket must match every condition field you fill in (company, group, product, source, ticket type and tag) and any value inside a field. Empty fields match any ticket. Tickets that match no policy are left alone. You can have up to 20 policies.

How are due times worked out?

From the time the ticket was created plus the target for its priority (Low, Medium, High or Urgent), up to 90 days. With business hours, only the open hours of the policy's time zone count, and holidays are skipped. With Pause while Pending on, time the ticket spends in Pending does not count.

When are reminders and escalations sent?

A reminder goes out the number of minutes before a target that you set, up to 7 days. An escalation goes out at the breach, or the number of minutes after it that you set, up to 7 days. The app checks every 5 minutes, so a note can arrive up to about 5 minutes after its time. Each notice is sent once per ticket. If several are due at the same check, only the most serious one is sent.

Who is notified?

The note lists the assigned agent (if that option is on) and up to 10 email addresses from the policy as people to notify. Notes are private, so customers do not see them.

Which tickets are checked?

Open tickets updated in the last 30 days, up to the 500 most recently updated per check. Resolved and closed tickets get no notices. Each check sends up to 6 notices, oldest tickets first, and the rest follow on the next check.

Does a custom status pause the clock?

No. Only the Pending status pauses the clock. Pending time is recorded from the moment the app is installed.

Are next response targets included?

No. The app tracks first response and resolution targets.

What is in the breach report?

The reminders (at risk) and escalations (breached) the app sent, from its last 200 notices, filtered by a date window and kind, newest first, with counts and a CSV download with time, ticket id, target, notice, status and policy.

Can I stop all notices for a while?

Yes. Turn on Pause all notices on the admin page. Your policies are kept.

Why does the app ask for my API key?

Each user signs in once per session with their own Freshdesk API key (Profile settings > View API key). Freshdesk confirms who you are. The key is used for that one check and is not stored, and the session ends after 30 minutes. Any agent can see the ticket sidebar, and only admins (Account Administrator or Administrator role) can change policies, see recent notices and run the breach report.

A ticket did not get a notice.

Check that a policy matches the ticket, that its priority has a target, that the reminder or escalation is turned on, that the ticket was updated in the last 30 days and that notices are not paused. Then email us with the ticket id.
