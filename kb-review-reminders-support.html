Contact

Email: joesimo@mail.grokbot.com

Support is by email only. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include your Freshdesk domain, an article id and a screenshot of what you see. Never send your API key.

Frequently asked questions

How is the due date worked out?

The date the article was last marked reviewed in the app, or its last update in Freshdesk if it was never marked, plus its review interval. The interval comes from the article override, then the folder, then the category, then your default (180 days unless you change it). Intervals can be from 7 to 730 days.

When is an article due soon?

When its due date is within your lead time, 7 days by default. On the due date it becomes overdue.

Does Mark reviewed change my article?

No. The app stores the review date itself, so the article and its last update in Freshdesk stay as they are.

What does Snooze do?

It moves the article's due date 30 days later than a review today would.

Who gets the reminder ticket?

The agent who created the article. If that agent is missing or deactivated, or the ticket for them fails, the ticket goes unassigned to the default owner email you set in Settings. Without a default owner, those articles stay on the due list without a ticket. Each owner gets one ticket per check that lists their articles, and an article is reminded at most once when it becomes due soon and once when it becomes overdue for the same due date. Up to 4 owners get a ticket per check, and the rest follow on the next check.

Can I turn reminder tickets off?

Yes. Choose list only in Settings and the app only keeps the due list on its admin page.

Are draft articles checked?

Only if you turn on Include drafts. By default the app checks published articles.

How often does the check run?

Once a day. You can also select Run check now on the admin page. On large knowledge bases the daily check stops when it reaches its limit for one run and continues where it stopped on the next run.

Can I download the due list?

Yes. Export the due list as a CSV file with the article id, title, category id, folder id, owner agent id, due date and state.

Why does the app ask for my API key?

Each admin signs in once per session with their own Freshdesk API key (Profile settings > View API key). Freshdesk confirms who you are, so only admins (Account Administrator or Administrator role) can use the app. The key is used for that one check and is not stored, and the session ends after 30 minutes.

Articles are missing from the list.

Check that the article is published (or that Include drafts is on), that its due date falls within your lead time, and that the last check finished. Then email us with the article id.
