Snooze a Freshdesk ticket until a date and time

Ticket Snooze lets agents park a ticket until a chosen date and time. When that time comes, the app sets the ticket back to Open and adds a private note for the assignee, so nothing waits in the queue and nobody has to remember to come back.

Get help Read the privacy policy

Out of the queue, back on time

  • Pick a wake time

    In the ticket sidebar, pick a date and time in your local time, or use a quick choice: 1 hour, 4 hours, tomorrow or next week. Add an optional note.

  • Your snooze status

    The ticket moves to Pending, or to a custom Snoozed status with the SLA timer off, and gets a tag such as "snoozed".

  • Wakes on the date

    At the wake time the app sets the ticket back to Open, removes the tag and adds a private note. The assignee can be alerted by email.

  • Leaves moved tickets alone

    If the customer replied, someone changed the status or the ticket was deleted, the app does not touch it and simply ends the snooze.

  • See every snooze

    The admin page lists every snoozed ticket with its wake time and the agent who snoozed it, and wakes any of them early.

  • Retries on its own

    A background check every 10 minutes retries any wake that failed, up to 5 times.

How it works

  1. Install Ticket Snooze from the Freshworks Marketplace and enter your Freshdesk domain and an admin API key. The app checks both before it finishes.
  2. Open Ticket Snooze from the apps menu and choose the snooze status, wake status and tag.
  3. Open any ticket, pick a wake time in the Ticket Snooze sidebar and select Snooze.

The soonest wake time is 5 minutes ahead and the latest is one year ahead. Up to 500 tickets can be snoozed at the same time per helpdesk.

Private by design

Ticket Snooze runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. Your API key is stored as an encrypted installation parameter and is used only to call your own Freshdesk account. No data leaves Freshworks. Read the full privacy policy.
