SLA Policies Plus privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what data the SLA Policies Plus app for Freshdesk ("the app") processes and how. The app is published on the Freshworks Marketplace.

Summary

  • The app runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party.
  • The app stores its policies, Pending pauses and sent notices per ticket, a log of recent notices and short sign-in sessions. It stores no ticket subjects, ticket text or customer contact data.

Data the app processes

  1. Installation settings. Your Freshdesk domain, stored by Freshworks as an installation parameter.
  2. Freshdesk API key. Stored by Freshworks as a secure (encrypted) installation parameter. It is used only to call the Freshdesk API of your own account, and it is never shown in the app or written to logs.
  3. Policies. In the Freshworks app key-value store attached to your installation: each policy's name, conditions (company, group and product ids, sources, ticket types and tags), targets per priority, hours (time zone, open hours per weekday and holiday dates), notice options and the notice email addresses you enter (up to 10 per policy), plus the pause switch and the time of the last save.
  4. Ticket records. In the same store, per ticket the app has worked with: the times the ticket entered and left Pending (up to 12 pauses) and the times each reminder or escalation was sent. Each record is removed 120 days after its last update.
  5. Notice log. In the same store: the last 200 notices the app sent, each with the time, ticket id, notice kind and policy id. Older entries are overwritten. The breach report and the recent notices list are built from this log.
  6. Check summary. In the same store: the time and counts of the last check, the position in the notice log, and up to 300 ticket ids with the next time each needs a look and its last update time, so the app does not read the same ticket again too early.
  7. Your own API key at sign-in. Each user signs in once per session with their own Freshdesk API key. The app sends it only to your Freshdesk domain to ask who owns it (agent id, name, email and roles), then discards it. The key is never stored, logged or shown again.
  8. Sign-in sessions. In the key-value store: a random session token with the agent's id, name, email and whether they are an admin, removed after 30 minutes.
  9. Data read from your helpdesk. Every 5 minutes the app reads the tickets updated in the last 30 days with their stats (id, status, priority, created and updated times, company, group, product, source, type, tags, assigned agent, and first response and resolution times). To notify the assigned agent it reads that agent's email. The policy editor reads your group and company names, the sidebar reads the open ticket with its stats, and sign-in reads the roles list. None of this is stored apart from the records above.

What the app changes

When a reminder or escalation is due, the app adds a private note to the ticket with the policy name and the due time, and lists the assigned agent (if that option is on) and the email addresses from the policy as people to notify, so Freshdesk can email them. When the policy asks for it, the app adds the tag sla-at-risk or sla-breached and keeps the ticket's other tags. The app does not change Freshdesk's own SLA policies or due dates, does not change a ticket's status, priority, group or assignment, and does not reply to customers.

Third parties and external resources

The app does not send data to any third party and makes no requests outside your Freshdesk account. Notification emails for its notes are sent by Freshdesk. The 5-minute check runs on the Freshworks scheduling service. Apart from the Freshworks app client script that every Freshworks app loads, the interface uses no external scripts, fonts, analytics, cookies or trackers.

Retention and deletion

Sign-in sessions are removed after 30 minutes. Ticket records are removed 120 days after their last update. The notice log keeps the last 200 notices. Policies stay until you change them or uninstall the app. Notes and tags the app added stay in your Freshdesk until you delete them. When the app is uninstalled, it removes its 5-minute schedule, and Freshworks removes the installation parameters and the app's stored data according to the Freshworks platform policies.

Security

All communication with the Freshdesk API uses HTTPS and is performed by the Freshworks platform. The app sends requests only to the Freshdesk domain you entered, after checking it. Every request to the app's server functions needs a verified sign-in session. Any agent can see the ticket sidebar, and only Freshdesk admins (Account Administrator or Administrator role) can change policies, see the recent notices or run the breach report.

Your rights

Because the app does not keep personal data outside your Freshworks account, requests about personal data in your helpdesk should be handled in Freshdesk. For questions about this policy, contact us at the email below.
