Store reviews as Freshdesk tickets

App Reviews brings your Google Play and Apple App Store reviews into Freshdesk, so your team can triage and answer them next to every other customer conversation, and reply to the store without leaving the ticket.

Get help Read the privacy policy

Every review, one ticket

  • Hourly import

    Every hour the app checks each app you list and creates one ticket per new review, with the rating, nickname, title, text, app version, device, OS, language and a store link.

  • Ready for views and SLAs

    Tickets are tagged store:google-play or store:app-store and stars:1 to stars:5, and low ratings get a higher priority.

  • Reply from the ticket

    Send a public reply on a review ticket and the app posts it to the store as your developer response, then adds a private note with the result.

  • Live character counter

    The ticket sidebar shows the store, the rating and how many characters the store allows, so agents see the limit while they write.

  • Edited reviews

    When a Google Play reviewer edits a review, the app adds a private note with the new rating and text to the same ticket instead of opening a new one.

  • Both stores, safely

    Up to 5 Google Play apps and 5 App Store apps per installation. One store failing never stops the other, and each review becomes exactly one ticket.

How it works

  1. Install App Reviews from the Freshworks Marketplace and enter your Freshdesk domain and an admin API key.
  2. Add a Google service account key with Reply to reviews access, an App Store Connect API key with the Customer Support role, or both, and list your apps.
  3. New reviews arrive as tickets within the hour. Answer with a public reply and a private note confirms whether the store accepted it.

Each run creates at most 15 new tickets to respect API limits; the rest come in on the next run. Google Play's API only returns reviews from roughly the last week, so older reviews are not imported at install.

Private by design

App Reviews runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. Your store keys and API key are stored as encrypted installation parameters and are used only to call Google Play, App Store Connect and your own Freshdesk. There is no outside server and no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
