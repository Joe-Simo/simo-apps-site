Google reviews as Freshdesk tickets

Business Reviews brings the Google reviews of your business locations into Freshdesk, so your team can triage and answer them next to every other customer conversation, and post the owner reply without leaving the ticket.

Setup guide and help Read the privacy policy

Every review, one ticket

  • Hourly import

    Every hour the app checks each location you pick and creates one ticket per new review, with the location, rating, reviewer name, text, any current owner reply and a link to the review on Google.

  • Ready for views and SLAs

    Tickets are tagged gbp, stars:1 to stars:5 and location:ID, and 1 and 2 star reviews get high priority.

  • Reply from the ticket

    Send a public reply on a review ticket and the app posts it on Google as the owner reply, then adds a private note with the result.

  • Live byte counter

    The ticket sidebar shows the rating, the location and a counter against Google's 4096 byte limit. Agents can post a draft from the sidebar or open it in the reply editor.

  • Edits and rejected replies

    When a reviewer changes a review, the app adds a private note to the same ticket. When Google rejects an owner reply, a note gives the reason Google reports.

  • Pick your locations

    Sign in with Google, load your Business Profile locations and tick up to 10. Each review becomes exactly one ticket, even across runs.

How it works

  1. Get Business Profile API access for your own Google Cloud project and create an OAuth client. The setup guide covers each step.
  2. Install Business Reviews from the Freshworks Marketplace, sign in with Google, enter your Freshdesk domain and an admin API key, and pick your locations.
  3. Reviews from the last 14 days arrive as tickets within the hour. Answer with a public reply and a private note confirms whether Google accepted it.

Google must approve your Cloud project for Business Profile API access before any app can read reviews. Google asks for a profile verified and active for 60 days or more, with a website. Each run creates at most 15 new tickets; the rest come in on the next run.

Private by design

Business Reviews runs entirely on the Freshworks platform and uses your own Google Cloud project and OAuth client. Your Freshdesk API key is stored as an encrypted installation parameter. The app talks only to Google's Business Profile APIs and your own Freshdesk. There is no outside server and no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
