Send Later privacy policy

Send Later is a Freshdesk app that runs entirely on the Freshworks platform.

What the app stores

For each scheduled message: the ticket number, message text, Cc and Bcc addresses, chosen time and time zone, the scheduling agent's id and name, the options chosen, and the delivery result. This is stored in the Freshworks key-value store of your own installation. Finished messages are kept as a short history per ticket.

What the app reads

The ticket's status and conversations (to skip a reply when the customer answered first and to confirm delivery) and the scheduling agent's signature, only when "Add my signature" is selected.

Where data goes

Only to your own Freshdesk account, through the Freshdesk API. The app sends no data to the developer or to any third party, and uses no analytics, cookies or trackers.

API key

The admin API key you enter at installation is stored by Freshworks as an encrypted, secure installation parameter. The developer cannot see it.

Removal

Uninstalling the app removes its stored data through the Freshworks platform.
