Ticket Approvals privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what data the Ticket Approvals app for Freshdesk ("the app") processes and how. The app is published on the Freshworks Marketplace.

Summary

  • The app runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party.
  • The app stores only your settings, your rules and the approval requests on your tickets.

Data the app processes

  1. Installation settings. Your Freshdesk domain, stored by Freshworks as an installation parameter.
  2. Freshdesk API key. Stored by Freshworks as a secure (encrypted) installation parameter. It is used only to call the Freshdesk API of your own account, and it is never shown in the app or written to logs.
  3. Settings and rules. In the Freshworks app key-value store attached to your installation: the ticket statuses you map, whether approvers are notified, and each rule (name, ticket types, group numbers, priorities, tags, number field and threshold, approvers and reason).
  4. Approval requests. In the same key-value store, per ticket with a request: the ticket number, the requester's and approvers' agent numbers and names, the rule (any one or all), reason, amount, each decision with its comment and time, and the outcome. The newest 5 requests per ticket are kept.
  5. Open request list and decision log. A list of open requests (up to 1,000) and a log of roughly the last 1,000 events (time, ticket and request numbers, event, agent number and name, rule, amount and comment) for the full page view and CSV export.
  6. Data read from your helpdesk. The app reads agents (name, email and whether they are active) to verify approvers and the acting agent, and the type, group, priority, tags and custom fields of tickets to apply rules. Approver emails are used only to fill the notify list of the private note and are not shown in the browser.

What the app changes

For each request, decision and cancel, the app adds one private note to the ticket. If you choose statuses under Settings, it sets the ticket status. The app does not close or delete tickets.

Third parties and external resources

The app does not send data to any third party and makes no requests outside your Freshdesk account. Notification emails are sent by Freshdesk itself. Apart from the Freshworks app client script that every Freshworks app loads, the interface uses no external scripts, fonts, analytics, cookies or trackers.

Retention and deletion

Settings, rules and requests stay in your installation until you change or delete them or uninstall the app. Private notes added by the app stay on the tickets like any other note. When the app is uninstalled, Freshworks removes the installation parameters and the app's stored data according to the Freshworks platform policies.

Security

All communication with the Freshdesk API uses HTTPS and is performed by the Freshworks platform. Every decision is checked on the server against the acting agent.

Your rights

Because the app does not keep personal data outside your Freshworks account, requests about personal data in your helpdesk should be handled in Freshdesk. For questions about this policy, contact us at the email below.
