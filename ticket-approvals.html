Approvals on Freshdesk tickets

Ticket Approvals adds a real approval step to Freshdesk tickets for refunds, credits, discounts, policy exceptions and chargeable work. Freshdesk's built-in approval workflow covers knowledge base articles only, so teams ask for sign-off in private notes and lose track of who said yes.

Sign-off you can find later

  • Ask from the ticket

    In the sidebar, pick 1 to 5 approvers, choose whether any one or all of them must approve, and add a reason and an optional amount.

  • Approve or reject

    Approvers see the request on the ticket and decide with a comment. A reject always needs a comment, and nobody can approve their own request.

  • Audit trail on the ticket

    Every request, decision and cancel is written to the ticket as a private note, and Freshdesk emails the approvers through the note's notify list.

  • Status that follows

    Choose a status for tickets while they wait, and others for approved and rejected, or leave the status as it is.

  • Automatic rules

    Up to 10 rules open a request when a ticket matches a type, group, priority or tag, or when a number field such as a refund amount crosses a threshold.

  • Queue and CSV log

    The full page view shows the requests waiting for you, all open requests and a decision log you can download as CSV.

How it works

  1. Install Ticket Approvals from the Freshworks Marketplace and enter your Freshdesk domain and an admin API key.
  2. Open a ticket and use the Ticket Approvals sidebar to request approval, or open the app from the apps menu to set statuses and rules.
  3. Approvers decide on the ticket. The decision is recorded as a private note and in the decision log.

Each decision is checked on the server against the acting agent, so only listed, active approvers can decide. The app records approvals; it does not issue refunds or credits itself.

Private by design

Ticket Approvals runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. Your API key is stored as an encrypted installation parameter and is used only to call your own Freshdesk account. No data leaves Freshworks. Read the full privacy policy.
