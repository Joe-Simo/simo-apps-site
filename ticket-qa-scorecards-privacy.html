Ticket QA Scorecards privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what data the Ticket QA Scorecards app for Freshdesk ("the app") processes and how. The app is published on the Freshworks Marketplace.

Summary

  • The app runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party.
  • The app stores your rubric and settings, the reviews your reviewers save, monthly totals, weekly review queues, a short list of agent names and short sign-in sessions. It reads ticket text and conversations to show them to reviewers, but does not store them.

Data the app processes

  1. Installation settings. Your Freshdesk domain, stored by Freshworks as an installation parameter.
  2. Freshdesk API key. The API key of an admin agent, stored by Freshworks as a secure (encrypted) installation parameter. It is used only to call the Freshdesk API of your own account, and it is never shown in the app or written to logs.
  3. Settings and rubric. In the Freshworks app key-value store attached to your installation: the rubric lines with their labels, help text, weights, scales and critical flags, each saved rubric version, the reviewer ids you pick, the sample settings (number per reviewer, minimum conversations and excluded tags), the pass mark and the agent view option.
  4. Reviews. In the same store, one record per reviewed ticket: the ticket id, the agent and group ids of the ticket, the reviewer id, the rubric version, the score for each line, the percent and pass or fail result, the reviewer's comment, the time, whether the agent acknowledged the review, and the agent's dispute comment with its time if they disputed it. Scoring a ticket again replaces its earlier review.
  5. Monthly totals and index. In the same store, for each month: totals per agent and per rubric line for the report, and the list of ticket ids reviewed that month (up to 600) for the CSV download.
  6. Weekly queues. In the same store: the ticket ids picked for review each week, with the agent, the assigned reviewer and whether the review is done, plus progress of a sample that is still being built.
  7. Agent names. In the same store: the ids and names (up to 40 characters) of your agents and whether each is active, so reviews can show names instead of ids. The list is refreshed after an hour.
  8. Your own API key at sign-in. Each person signs in once per session with their own Freshdesk API key. The app sends it only to your Freshdesk domain to ask who owns it (agent id, name, email and roles), then discards it. The key is never stored, logged or shown again.
  9. Sign-in sessions. In the key-value store: a random session token with the agent's id, name, email and whether they are an admin, removed after 30 minutes.
  10. Data read from your helpdesk. To build the weekly sample the app reads agents and searches for tickets resolved or closed in the last 7 days, with their agent, tags and number of conversations. When a reviewer opens a ticket to score it, the app reads its subject, description and conversations and shows them on the review screen. At sign-in it reads the agent who owns the key and the roles list. Ticket subjects, descriptions and conversations are never stored by the app.

What the app changes

Nothing in your helpdesk. The app does not change, reply to, tag or add notes to tickets, and does not change agents or settings in Freshdesk. It writes only to its own key-value store.

Who can see reviews

Admins see the report and the CSV download of all reviews. Admins and reviewers can open the review of a ticket in the ticket sidebar, and see their review queue and the tickets in it (admins see every reviewer's queue). When agent view is on, the agent who handled a ticket can see that ticket's review in the ticket sidebar, and no other agent can. These checks are made on the app's server side from the signed-in session.

Third parties and external resources

The app does not send data to any third party and makes no requests outside your Freshdesk account. The weekly sampler runs on the Freshworks scheduling service. Apart from the Freshworks app client script that every Freshworks app loads, the interface uses no external scripts, fonts, analytics, cookies or trackers.

Retention and deletion

Reviews, monthly totals, the monthly index, the rubric versions and the settings are kept for as long as the app is installed. This version of the app has no automatic expiry and no delete button. Weekly queues are deleted automatically two to three weeks after their week. Agent names are refreshed after an hour and sign-in sessions are removed after 30 minutes. Admins can download any month's reviews as a CSV file from the Report tab at any time, for example before uninstalling; files you download are on your own computer and are yours to keep or delete. When the app is uninstalled, it removes its schedule, and Freshworks deletes the installation parameters and all of the app's stored data for your account, which cannot be recovered. To erase all review data, uninstall the app.

Security

All communication with the Freshdesk API uses HTTPS and is performed by the Freshworks platform. The app sends requests only to the Freshdesk domain you entered, after checking it. Every request to the app's server functions needs a verified sign-in session. Only Freshdesk admins (Account Administrator or Administrator role) can change settings, run the sampler, see the report or download exports. Text in CSV files that a spreadsheet could read as a formula is prefixed with a single quote.

Your rights

Because the app does not keep personal data outside your Freshworks account, requests about personal data in your helpdesk should be handled in Freshdesk. To remove an agent's reviews, uninstall the app after downloading the months you need. For questions about this policy, contact us at the email below.
