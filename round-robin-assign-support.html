Round Robin Assign support

Contact

Email: joesimo@mail.grokbot.com

Support is by email only. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include your Freshdesk domain, the group, a ticket number and a screenshot of what you see. Never send your API key.

Frequently asked questions

Which tickets does the app assign?

New tickets in a group you manage that have no agent yet and are not spam. When you turn on the option, it also assigns a ticket that is moved into a managed group without an agent. It never replaces an agent who is already set.

What is the difference between Round robin and Fewest today?

Round robin gives each new ticket to the next agent in your order and then moves the turn on. Fewest today gives it to the available agent with the fewest tickets from the app today, and ties go by order. Today's counts reset at midnight in the time zone you choose for the group.

When does an agent skip a turn?

When they are out of queue, outside their shift hours, or at their cap of open and pending tickets. An agent with no shift hours is always on shift, and an empty cap means no cap.

How do agents go in or out of queue?

On any ticket, select Round Robin Assign in the top bar, sign in with your own API key and use the switch. It applies to every managed group you belong to. Admins can switch any agent under Queue today on the admin page.

What happens to tickets when nobody is available?

They stay unassigned. A background check every 5 minutes assigns waiting tickets, oldest first, as soon as an agent is free. Before it assigns a ticket it reads it again, so a ticket someone assigned by hand in the meantime is left alone.

How many tickets can it assign?

About 8 assignments per minute per helpdesk, which is set by the Freshworks app storage limits. Tickets above that stay unassigned for a few minutes and are picked up by the background check, up to 5 per check.

How are caps counted?

The background check reads each capped agent's open and pending tickets from Freshdesk every 5 minutes. Tickets the app assigns in between count right away. A cap can be from 1 to 500 tickets.

What if a ticket turns out to be spam or is deleted?

If a ticket the app assigned is marked as spam or deleted within a day, the agent gets the turn back and today's count goes down. Merged tickets are not detected and still count.

Can I see who got which ticket and why?

Yes. The admin page shows who is next in each group, today's counts and the recent assignments with their result, and you can download the last 200 as a CSV file. You can also turn on a private note on each assigned ticket that names the agent and the reason.

Why does the app ask for my API key?

Everyone signs in once per session with their own Freshdesk API key (Profile settings > View API key). Freshdesk confirms who you are, so agents can only switch their own queue status and only admins can change the settings. The key is used for that one check and is not stored, and the session ends after 30 minutes.

Who can change the settings?

Only Freshdesk admins (Account Administrator or Administrator role) can open the admin page sections: groups, options, rotations, Queue today and the assignment log. Custom roles are not accepted as admins. Role changes apply at the next sign-in.

How do I stop the app for a while?

On the admin page, tick "Pause all assignment for now" and save. New tickets stay unassigned until you turn it off. Uninstalling the app removes its background check.

Tickets are not being assigned.

Check that the group is ticked under Groups, that its rotation has agents who are in queue and on shift, and that assignment is not paused. Then check that the API key in the app settings belongs to an active admin agent, and email us with a ticket number if it continues.
