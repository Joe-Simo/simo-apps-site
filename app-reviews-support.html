App Reviews support

Contact

Email: joesimo@mail.grokbot.com

Support is by email only. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include your Freshdesk domain, the ticket number and a screenshot of the private note or message you see. Never send your API key, service account key or .p8 key.

Frequently asked questions

How often are reviews imported?

Every hour. Each run creates up to 15 new tickets; any extra reviews are created on the next run.

Why are older Google Play reviews missing?

Google Play's API only returns reviews created or changed in roughly the last week, so reviews older than that at install time are not imported.

Which App Store reviews are imported?

The newest 50 reviews of each app are checked on every run, and each review becomes one ticket.

Who is the requester on review tickets?

Neither store shares reviewer email addresses, so tickets use a placeholder requester for each store (google-play-reviews@example.com and app-store-reviews@example.com). The reviewer's nickname is shown in the ticket. If your helpdesk sends automatic acknowledgement emails, you can exclude these two requesters in your automation rules.

How do I answer a review?

Send a public reply on the review ticket. The app posts it to the store as your developer response and adds a private note saying whether the store accepted it. Private notes are never posted.

Why was my reply not posted?

The private note on the ticket explains the reason: the reply was empty, longer than the store allows (Google Play allows about 350 characters), the store keys are missing, or the store returned an error. Fix the cause and send the reply again.

What happens when a reviewer edits a Google Play review?

The app adds a private note with the new rating and text to the existing ticket instead of creating a new ticket.

What access do the store keys need?

Google Play: a service account invited in Play Console with the Reply to reviews permission. App Store Connect: an API key with the Customer Support role or higher.
