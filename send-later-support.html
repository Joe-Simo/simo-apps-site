Send Later support

Contact

Email joesimo@mail.grokbot.com. Support is provided by email only, and we aim to reply within 2 business days. Please include your Freshdesk domain and the ticket number, never your API key.

Frequently asked questions

Whose name is on a scheduled reply?

The agent who scheduled it. The admin API key from setup is only used to post it.

Which time zone is used?

The time zone of the browser of the agent who schedules the message. It is shown under the date picker.

Why was my message "Not sent"?

By default a reply is not sent if the customer replied after you scheduled it, or if the ticket was resolved, closed, deleted or marked as spam. The reason is shown in the ticket's list. You can switch these checks off under Options before scheduling.

How precise is the timing?

Messages normally go out within a minute of the chosen time. If Freshdesk is busy, a background check every 5 minutes sends anything that is due.

Can a reply be sent twice?

No. If Freshdesk does not confirm a send, Send Later checks the ticket's conversation before retrying. After 5 failed attempts the message is marked Failed with the reason, and you can schedule it again.

Is there a limit?

Up to 900 messages can be waiting per helpdesk at once. Messages can be scheduled up to 12 months ahead.

What happens if I uninstall?

Waiting messages are not sent, and the app's stored data is removed by the Freshworks platform.
