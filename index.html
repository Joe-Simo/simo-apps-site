Careful apps for Freshdesk

Four small, focused apps for Freshdesk teams by independent developer Joe Simo. Each one runs entirely on the Freshworks platform, shows you what it will do before it does it, and is billed through the Freshworks Marketplace.

The apps

  • Retention Guard

    Ticket retention with a dry-run report first, legal hold, an audit log, and moves to Trash only. $19 per account per month.

  • Bulk Update Pro

    Update up to 5,000 tickets per job by field name, with a preview, background batches and a safe undo. $19 per account per month.

  • Send Later: Schedule Replies

    Schedule replies and private notes, skipped automatically if the customer answers first. $1.50 per agent per month.

  • Recurring Tickets

    Create the same ticket every day, weekday, week, month or few days, with any ticket fields, a preview of the next dates and a run history. $19 per account per month.
