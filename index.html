Careful apps for Freshdesk and Freshservice

Twelve small, focused apps for Freshdesk and Freshservice teams by independent developer Joe Simo. Each one runs entirely on the Freshworks platform, shows you what it will do before it does it, and is billed through the Freshworks Marketplace.

The apps

  • Retention Guard

    Ticket retention with a dry-run report first, legal hold, an audit log, and moves to Trash only. $19 per account per month.

  • Bulk Update Pro

    Update up to 5,000 tickets per job by field name, with a preview, background batches and a safe undo. $19 per account per month.

  • Send Later: Schedule Replies

    Schedule replies and private notes, skipped automatically if the customer answers first. $1.50 per agent per month.

  • Recurring Tickets

    Create the same ticket every day, weekday, week, month or few days, with any ticket fields, a preview of the next dates and a run history. $19 per account per month.

  • Ticket Checklists

    Step by step checklists on tickets, added automatically by type, group, priority or tag, with required steps before Resolve or Close. $19 per account per month.

  • Privacy Requests

    Export a customer's profile, tickets and conversations to one file, erase a contact on request, and review long-inactive contacts, with a removal log. $19 per account per month.

  • Bulk Update Pro for Freshservice

    Update up to 5,000 Freshservice tickets per job by field name, with a preview, private notes, presets, background batches and a safe undo. $19 per account per month.

  • Ticket Approvals

    Ask named agents to approve refunds, credits and exceptions on a ticket, with automatic rules, status changes, a private note audit trail and a CSV decision log. $19 per account per month.

  • App Reviews

    Bring Google Play and Apple App Store reviews into Freshdesk as tickets every hour, and reply from the ticket to post your developer response to the store. $29 per account per month.

  • Business Reviews

    Bring the Google reviews of your business locations into Freshdesk as tickets every hour, and reply from the ticket to post the owner reply on Google. $29 per account per month.

  • Ticket Snooze

    Snooze a ticket until a date and time, then set it back to Open with a private note and an optional alert for the assignee. $19 per account per month.

  • CSV Ticket Updater

    Give every ticket its own new values from a spreadsheet, with a preview that checks every row, background batches, a change log and a safe undo. $19 per account per month.
