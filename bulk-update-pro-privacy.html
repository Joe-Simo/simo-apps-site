Bulk Update Pro privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what data the Bulk Update Pro app for Freshdesk ("the app") processes and how. The app is published on the Freshworks Marketplace.

Summary

  • The app runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party.
  • We do not store ticket conversations, descriptions, attachments or contact details.

Data the app processes

  1. Installation settings. Your Freshdesk domain, stored by Freshworks as an installation parameter.
  2. Freshdesk API key. Stored by Freshworks as a secure (encrypted) installation parameter. It is used only to call the Freshdesk API of your own account, and it is never shown in the app or written to logs.
  3. Ticket data read from your helpdesk. To preview and apply changes, the app reads tickets you list through the Freshdesk API, together with your ticket field definitions, group names and agent names and emails. The preview shows up to 5 ticket subjects. This happens inside the Freshworks serverless environment, and subjects, names and emails are not stored.
  4. Jobs and change log. In the Freshworks app key-value store attached to your installation, the app keeps the ticket numbers of each job, the changes you requested, and one log entry per ticket: the ticket number, the result, the previous and new values of the fields the job changed (these can include agent and group IDs, tags and the values of custom fields you chose to change) and any error message from Freshdesk. For each job the app also keeps the display names of the agents, groups and statuses involved and the labels of the fields it changes, so the log shows names instead of IDs. The 9 most recent jobs are listed; storage of older jobs is reused and overwritten by new jobs.

What the app changes

The app only changes the tickets and fields you list, and only after you start a job from its preview. Undo changes the same tickets back.

Third parties and external resources

The app does not send data to any third party and makes no requests outside your Freshdesk account. The interface uses the Freshworks Crayons components, bundled inside the app package, so no external scripts, fonts or icons are downloaded at runtime.

Retention and deletion

Jobs and change logs stay in your installation until they are overwritten by newer jobs or the app is uninstalled. When the app is uninstalled, Freshworks removes the installation parameters and the app's key-value data according to the Freshworks platform policies. Download any change log you need as CSV before uninstalling.

Security

All communication with the Freshdesk API uses HTTPS and is performed by the Freshworks platform. Access to the app follows your Freshdesk agent permissions.

Your rights

Because the app does not keep personal data outside your Freshworks account, requests about personal data in your helpdesk should be handled in Freshdesk. For questions about this policy, contact us at the email below.
