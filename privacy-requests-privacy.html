Privacy Requests privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what data the Privacy Requests app for Freshdesk ("the app") processes and how. The app is published on the Freshworks Marketplace.

Summary

  • The app runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party.
  • The app stores only its settings and a log of removals, without the names or emails of removed people.

Data the app processes

  1. Installation settings. Your Freshdesk domain, stored by Freshworks as an installation parameter.
  2. Freshdesk API key. Stored by Freshworks as a secure (encrypted) installation parameter. It is used only to call the Freshdesk API of your own account, and it is never shown in the app or written to logs.
  3. Settings. In the Freshworks app key-value store attached to your installation: the number of months for the inactive rule, the keep tag and whether removals delete or erase.
  4. Removal log. In the same store, up to 200 entries: the time, the kind of removal, the action, the contact number, the number of tickets, the reference typed by the admin, the cutoff date and the name of the admin who did it. The name and email of the removed person are not stored.
  5. Data read from your helpdesk. When an admin uses the app, it reads contacts (by email, or those not updated since the cutoff), the tickets and conversations of a contact, and the list of agents. This data is shown on screen or put in an export file that is downloaded to the admin's computer. It is not stored by the app.

What the app changes

The app deletes contacts only when an admin asks it to: a soft delete (restorable from Deleted contacts) or a permanent delete of the contact and their tickets. It does not edit tickets or contacts.

Third parties and external resources

The app does not send data to any third party and makes no requests outside your Freshdesk account. Apart from the Freshworks app client script that every Freshworks app loads, the interface uses no external scripts, fonts, analytics, cookies or trackers.

Retention and deletion

Settings and the log stay in your installation until the app is uninstalled. The log keeps the newest 200 entries. Export files are saved on the admin's computer and are the admin's responsibility. When the app is uninstalled, Freshworks removes the installation parameters and the app's stored data according to the Freshworks platform policies.

Security

All communication with the Freshdesk API uses HTTPS and is performed by the Freshworks platform. The app calls the API with the key entered at installation, so install it with an admin key and give access to the app only to people who handle privacy requests.

Your rights

The app is a tool for your team to carry out requests about personal data in your helpdesk. The decision whether to export or erase data stays with you. For questions about this policy, contact us at the email below.
