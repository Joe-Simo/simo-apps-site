Schedule Freshdesk replies for the right moment

Send Later adds a panel to the Freshdesk ticket sidebar. Write a reply or a private note, pick a time in your own time zone, and it goes out on schedule under your name. If the customer answers first, it is not sent.

Get help Read the privacy policy

Follow-ups that never embarrass you

  • Quick picks

    In 1 hour, Tomorrow 9 AM, Monday 9 AM, or any date and time, always in the agent's own time zone.

  • Skips when it should

    By default a reply is not sent if the customer replied since you scheduled it, or if the ticket was resolved, closed, deleted or marked as spam. The reason is shown on the ticket.

  • Full control per ticket

    See what is waiting and what went out. Send now, change the time or cancel any waiting message.

  • Reply options

    Cc and Bcc, your agent signature, and an optional status to set after sending, such as Pending after a follow-up.

  • Never sends twice

    If Freshdesk does not confirm a send, the app checks the ticket before trying again.

  • Dependable delivery

    Every message has its own timed event, with a background check every 5 minutes as a backup and automatic retries.

How it works

  1. Install Send Later from the Freshworks Marketplace and enter your Freshdesk domain and an admin API key.
  2. Open any ticket and find Send Later in the sidebar.
  3. Write the message, choose a time, and click Schedule reply or Schedule note.

Private by design

Send Later runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. Your API key is stored as an encrypted installation parameter and is used only to call your own Freshdesk account. No data leaves Freshworks. Read the full privacy policy.
