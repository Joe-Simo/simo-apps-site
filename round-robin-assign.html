Round robin ticket assignment for Freshdesk

Round Robin Assign hands out new tickets in your Freshdesk groups automatically, one agent at a time. Freshdesk includes round robin routing on Pro and Enterprise; on plans without it, such as Growth, this app does the handing out for you, so loads stay even and no ticket waits for a lead to notice it.

Fair turns, without a lead handing them out

  • Next in turn

    Each new ticket in a managed group goes to the next agent in your order. Or choose Fewest today, which picks the agent with the fewest tickets from the app today.

  • Skips who is away

    Agents skip their turn while out of queue, outside their weekly shift hours or at their cap of open and pending tickets.

  • Agents set their own status

    From the ticket top bar, agents switch themselves in or out of queue. Admins can switch anyone on the admin page.

  • Nothing left behind

    Tickets that arrive while nobody is available are assigned by a check every 5 minutes, oldest first. A ticket someone assigned by hand is left alone.

  • Fair counts

    If an assigned ticket is marked as spam or deleted within a day, the agent gets the turn back. Today's counts reset at midnight in the group's time zone.

  • See the queue

    Who is next, today's counts and the last 200 assignments with their result, with a CSV download. An optional private note names the agent on each ticket.

How it works

  1. Install Round Robin Assign from the Freshworks Marketplace and enter your Freshdesk domain and an admin API key. The app checks both before it finishes.
  2. Open Round Robin Assign from the apps menu, pick the groups to manage, and set each group's agents, order, mode, caps and shift hours.
  3. Agents sign in from the ticket top bar and switch themselves into the queue. New tickets in those groups are assigned automatically.

Round Robin Assign handles about 8 assignments per minute per helpdesk, a limit of the Freshworks app storage; tickets above that are picked up by the 5 minute check. It manages up to 50 groups with up to 100 agents per rotation. It does not route by skills, between groups, or for chat and calls.

Private by design

Round Robin Assign runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. Your API key is stored as an encrypted installation parameter and is used only to call your own Freshdesk account. Everyone signs in with their own API key, which is not stored, and only admins can change the settings. No data leaves Freshworks. Read the full privacy policy.
