Answer privacy requests from one page in Freshdesk

When a customer asks for a copy of their data or asks to be forgotten, Privacy Requests finds them by email, downloads their profile, tickets and conversations as one file, and erases the contact and their tickets when you confirm. It also finds contacts that have been inactive for years, so you can delete or erase them after a review.

Get help Read the privacy policy

Access, erasure and retention in one place

  • Find the person

    Enter the email of the person making the request. The app shows the contact, their details and how many tickets they raised.

  • One export file

    Download a JSON file with the profile, up to 5,000 tickets and the conversations on them. Private notes stay out unless you include them.

  • Erase with confirmation

    Type a request reference and the contact's email again, then erase. The contact and all their tickets are deleted permanently.

  • Inactive contacts

    Choose 6 to 120 months. The app lists contacts with no updates and no ticket activity since then, leaving out agents and contacts with your keep tag.

  • Delete or erase

    Delete moves contacts to Deleted contacts, where they can be restored. Erase removes them and their tickets for good. Each contact is checked again first.

  • Removal log

    Every removal is logged with the time, contact number, ticket count, reference and admin. Names and emails of removed people are not kept. Download it as CSV.

How it works

  1. Install Privacy Requests from the Freshworks Marketplace and enter your Freshdesk domain and an admin API key.
  2. Open Privacy Requests from the apps menu and enter the email of the person making the request.
  3. Download the export, or erase the contact after typing the reference and their email.

Nothing is removed automatically. One scan lists up to 300 inactive contacts, a Freshdesk search limit; run it again after removing them. Erasing cannot be undone. You decide whether a request is valid.

Private by design

Privacy Requests runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. Your API key is stored as an encrypted installation parameter and is used only to call your own Freshdesk account. Exports are downloaded to your computer and are not sent anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
