Ticket retention for Freshdesk, without the risk

Retention Guard keeps your helpdesk inside your retention policy. It shows you exactly which tickets are in scope before anything changes, then moves old resolved or closed tickets to the Freshdesk Trash in small, audited batches. It never deletes permanently.

Get help Read the privacy policy

Built for data you cannot afford to lose

  • Report-only by default

    After install the app only scans and reports. Nothing changes until you review a complete dry-run report and type MOVE TO TRASH.

  • Legal hold always wins

    Tickets with the legal hold tag, an excluded tag, an excluded group, or a status outside your scope are never moved.

  • Re-checked before every move

    Each ticket is read again from Freshdesk and checked against every rule immediately before it is moved, so a ticket reopened or put on hold a moment ago is left alone.

  • Trash, not deletion

    Tickets go to the Freshdesk Trash, where an admin can restore them. The app never calls a permanent delete.

  • Full audit log

    Every ticket moved is recorded with its ID, status, group and timestamps. Browse it in the app or download it as CSV.

  • Gentle on your account

    Small batches every 5 minutes, well under Freshdesk API limits, with automatic backoff and exact resume after any interruption.

How it works

  1. Install Retention Guard from the Freshworks Marketplace and enter your Freshdesk domain and an admin API key.
  2. Choose the retention period, the statuses in scope and your exclusions: legal hold tag, excluded tags, excluded groups and an optional minimum ticket age.
  3. Review the dry-run report on the status page: how many tickets match, why others were excluded, and sample ticket links.
  4. When you are satisfied, enable Move to Trash and type the confirmation phrase. Changing any setting later returns the app to report-only mode.

Private by design

Retention Guard runs entirely on the Freshworks platform. Your API key is stored as an encrypted installation parameter and is used only to call your own Freshdesk account. The app stores no ticket subjects, content, attachments or contact details, and it makes no requests outside your Freshdesk account. Read the full privacy policy.
